# First-person weapons

One line per hero. What the hands hold, and why it fits the kit. Built by `vm_build.py`. Contact sheet from `sheet.py`.

- **Ah Ma (ahma)**: both hands on her walking frame with the red basket on the front bar. Walker Combo swings the walker. The frame keeps its full mesh, only the hands get thinned to meet the budget.
- **Stinky Uncle (uncle)**: right hand is a fist with the index finger out and a green booger on the tip (A). Left hand carries a kopi bag on a red string for Kopi Break. Booger Flick lobs the booger, so it hides during the throw. The newspaper is gone.
- **NSF (nsboy)**: a black toy rifle held level, barrel forward. A red rubber band runs along the top from the muzzle post to a wooden clothes peg (A). Spare bands sit round the fore-end. Rubber Band Rifle stretches and fires the band.
- **Engineer Encik (engineer)**: right hand holds a navy and lime foam dart blaster with a white primer knob, spare darts clipped on the side and one dart in the muzzle (A). Left hand carries the red toolbox by its handle, long face to the camera. Foam Dart Ricochet fires darts, the toolbox is where the turrets come from.
- **Gym Hunk (gym)**: both hands on a med ball. Med Ball Reps slams it.
- **Recess Rascal (rascal)**: right fist holds a folded paper shuriken (A). Left fist holds a plastic ruler. Paper Shuriken throws the star.
- **Head Prefect (debater)**: right hand holds a maroon megaphone. Left hand holds a gold desk bell. Strong Words are shouted through the megaphone.
- **Teh Tarik Auntie (tarik)**: steel pulling jug in the right hand, steel mug in the left. Tea Stream pours from the jug.
- **Delivery Driver (rider)**: right fist holds a fan of three disposable chopstick pairs, each half out of its paper sleeve. Left fist holds the spare bundle in a rubber band, with the order phone strapped to the forearm. Sun sleeves and green jacket cuffs match the toy. Chopstick Fan throws the fan.
- **Nightclub DJ (dj)**: left fist holds a microphone. Right hand rests on a launchpad with lit pads. Sound is the weapon.
- **Birdwatcher (lens)**: both hands on a camera with a long telephoto lens. Telephoto Shot charges and fires through it.
- **The Siaolang (siao)**: two big bare fists on thick forearms. Grubby cloth hand wraps, scraped red knuckles, a crossed plaster on each forearm. Bare Arms is a haymaker, so there is no weapon.
- **Ice Cream Uncle (ice)**: right hand holds a frost nozzle. Left hand holds an ice cream sandwich in wafers.

## Contract

Each GLB has meshes `R` (right arm and weapon), `L` (left arm) and `A` (ammo, only uncle, nsboy, engineer, rascal). Pivots and rest positions come from `P` and `rl` in the config and match the game. The wristband material is named `team`.
