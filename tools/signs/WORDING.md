# Sign wording (Chinese, Malay, Tamil) with English gloss

Confidence: H = standard, seen on real Singapore signage or menus. M = correct and natural, not checked against a specific sign. L = unsure, needs a native check.
Stall name is invented (Kim Seng 金成 is a generic Hokkien-style name, not a known brand).

## sign_tray
| Language | Text | Gloss | Conf |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | Please return your tray here. | | H |
| Chinese | 请将托盘归还此处。 | Please return the tray to this place. | M (NEA-style signs use 托盘 or 餐盘, both fine) |
| Malay | Sila pulangkan dulang anda di sini. | Please return your tray here. | M-H (dulang = tray, pulangkan = return) |
| Tamil | தயவுசெய்து உங்கள் தட்டை இங்கே திருப்பி வைக்கவும். | Please put your tray back here. | **L-M, needs a native check.** Grammar and spelling are standard. The phrasing is my own, real signs may say "திருப்பிக் கொடுக்கவும்" (give back) instead. |
| Thanks line | 谢谢 / Terima kasih / நன்றி | thank you | H |
